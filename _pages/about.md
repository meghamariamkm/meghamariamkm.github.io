I am a Research Intern at the [Centre for Visual Information Technology (CVIT)](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/), IIIT Hyderabad, where I am currently involved in a collaborative research project.

I completed my M.S. by Research in Computer Science at [CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/), under the supervision of Prof. C. V. Jawahar. My master's thesis, titled *Multimodal Methods for Educational Applications*, explored the use of multimodal AI in educational and scientific contexts. During my M.S., my research spanned three broad directions: aligning spoken explanations with visual content in presentations, evaluating text-to-video generation models for educational physics content, and studying fine-grained correspondence across different forms of scientific media, including text, figures, equations, and presentations.

Prior to this, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from [Model Engineering College (MEC)](https://www.mec.ac.in/).
